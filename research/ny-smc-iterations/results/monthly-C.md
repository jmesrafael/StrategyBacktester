# Check C month by month — three ways of ordering events inside a candle

$10 risk per trade, fees and slippage included, Bybit perp, 18 Oct 2023 → 17 Oct 2026. **Current** = the engine behind every earlier number. **Pessimistic** = worst case whenever one 15m candle holds both a good and a bad level, plus one extra tick on breakeven/tightened stops. **1m replay** = the same trades re-run minute by minute (the closest thing to what really happened). The three differ only where a trade had an ambiguous candle, so most months are identical.
