Verdict

  • Your live coins are already the best WIDE coins. Of 36 coins MEXC trades enough, 9 were positive in every year with WIDE: GALA, SOL, ADA, NEAR, ZEC, DOT, LINK, ONE, XTZ. The top 5 (GALA, SOL, ADA, NEAR, ZEC) are already live; the 6th live coin, ARB, is positive all 3 years it has existed (+3 / +12 / +31R).
  • Swapping coins in by hindsight does not help. The 6 best "positive every year" coins (live 5 + DOT instead of ARB) turn ₱1,000 on Jan 1 2022 into ₱366k (fall 55%), the live 6 into ₱405k (fall 48%).
  • "Positive every year so far" does carry forward for the BB family, but only partly. For WIDE, such a coin was positive the next year 82% of the time, other coins 61%. BB 1H 79% vs 60%, BB 4H v4 78% vs 67%; for I-CORE it barely works (64% vs 58%). So roughly 1 in 5 "every year positive" coins still loses the next year.
  • Picking coins that way each year, honestly (only earlier years), lost to the live coins badly: WIDE ₱1,000 from 2022 → ₱12k with a 83% fall, vs ₱405k on the live coins. It still beat 82% of random coin sets. The picks of 2023 (ETC, DOGE, FIL, BTC...) and coins like FIL later turned negative.
  • Why the live coins look so good: they beat every one of 200 random coin sets for every strategy. Part of that is real fit, part is that they were chosen (Oct-Nov 2026) after seeing these same years. Expect future coins, including today's, to do closer to the "average coin" column.
  • Suggestion: keep the live 6 on WIDE (and on BB). Do not add or remove coins on this evidence. If a coin is ever added, the candidates for WIDE are LINK (7/7, R ÷ fall 4.7) and DOT (6/6, 4.8), both with good volume; each still needs the stability rule check (adding must not make the bot's losing months or fall worse).

Read before trusting

  • R per coin uses the bots' own engines, Binance candles + funding, fee 0.05% + slippage 0.02% per side, entries from Jan 1 2020, exits to Nov 24 2026. A year counts for a coin only if it was listed by Feb 15 of that year.
  • Only coins with a MEXC contract and at least US$500k traded a day (MEXC snapshot Nov 12 2026) can be picked; live coins always. That leaves 36 of 90. Today's volume is itself hindsight for earlier years.
  • The money test starts Jan 1 2022 (picks need 2 earlier years), live sizing (2.5% / 2% / 1.5%), MEXC minimum orders, open trades valued hourly. Amounts are backtest compounding: compare lines, do not expect them.
  • Five test years is a small sample; one bad pick year (2023) drives much of the gap.
