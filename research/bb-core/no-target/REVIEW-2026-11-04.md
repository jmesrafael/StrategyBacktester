# BB-Core (no target): review on Nov 4, 2026, and the decision to run it untouched for 3-6 months

Written for the owner and for whoever reads this later. Everything here is re-runnable: `node review-2026-11-04.js` (output saved in `out-review-2026-11-04.txt`, monthly table in `monthly-r.csv`).
Backtest = bb-core's own engine (`lucidio-core.js`, no-target default), five coins (SOL, ZEC, NEAR, ADA, GALA), Binance 4h candles, fees + real funding. Data in `bb-core/data/` ends Oct 21-26, 2026, so months up to Sep 2026 are complete.

## 1. Where the live bot stands (read from the dashboard database, Nov 4, 2026)

- Source: Supabase project "Backtester", table `bot_events` (what the VPS pushes to the dashboard). The VPS itself was not reachable.
- **Daily compare** (BB-Core 00:40 UTC, I-Core 00:50 UTC): `match` every day Oct 24-29. Mismatch flags on Oct 30, Oct 31, Nov 2, Nov 3, each one trade. Candle diffs are 0 throughout. Re-run on Nov 3 22:23 UTC: `match: true` for both bots.
- **Cause of every flagged mismatch:** MEXC refused the entry order, code 6002 ("Your futures position-opening has been restricted"): one ZEC short (I-Core), one NEAR short (BB-Core). The backtest took those trades, the bot could not; the next signal then looked "live-only" because the backtest was still inside the refused trade (`follows-refused`). Logic and candles agree; the exchange did not allow the orders.
- **Live results:** BB-Core 7 closed trades since Oct 29, **-5.0R** (6 stops, 1 time exit +1.2R on the SOL short). I-Core 1 closed trade, -1.0R (ZEC stop, Nov 4). Several shorts open, not counted.
- Open items: find out why MEXC restricted position-opening (email / MEXC app) and whether it is permanent; the latest events (Nov 3 22:04 UTC) showed `missing_candle` for all five coins, most likely the 20:00 candle still forming at that moment (not confirmed).

## 3. Odds of bad stretches (history since 2022)

- A month with **zero winners**: 0 of 57. Longest losing streak: **20 trades**. 15 trades in a row all losing: 0.7% of 15-trade windows.
- A month **net negative**: 20 of 57 (35%). Never more than 2 negative months in a row.
- November: 2020 +5.6R, 2021 +58.9R, 2022 -2.6R, 2023 -0.5R, 2024 +36.0R, 2025 +13.9R. No seasonal edge; wide spread.
- **November 2026 projection** (started at -5R): median about 0R, 10th-90th percentile about -14R to +17R, about 45-50% chance of ending negative.
- **October 2026 was the worst month in the history (-16.6R, partial data)**, worse than Feb 2026 (-13.5R), Dec 2025 (-12.1R), Jul 2022 (-11.5R). The bot started in that stretch. A -5R opening is ordinary: 14% of all 7-trade windows are at -5R or worse; 16% of 30-day windows; -12R or worse is about 3%.

## 5. Decision: run it untouched for 3-6 months

Rolling windows of untouched running (any start day since 2022, no target, five coins):

| Run length | Trades (median) | Net R: 10th pct / median / 90th pct | Negative | Worse than -10R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 month | 18 | -7.4 / +5.5 / +22.1 | 36% | 5% |
| 3 months | 54 | -1.3 / +15.5 / +41.8 | 11% | 3% |
| 6 months | 109 | +10.2 / +34.2 / +56.2 | 1.6% | 0.3% |

- The backtest is profitable. The **live bot is not yet proven**: 8 closed trades is nothing. At 18 trades a month, 3 months is about 54 trades and 6 months about 109. The run log's own estimate for separating +0.3R per trade from luck is 150-300 trades (mid-to-late 2027), so 3-6 months can show **"live matches the backtest"**, not "the edge is real".
- Expected after 3 months: about +15R median, but about 1 in 9 such stretches ended negative. After 6 months: about +34R, negative only 1.6% of the time in the past. Past results do not guarantee the future.
- **Do not change risk, coins or exit rule** during the window (RUN-LOG Oct 26 / Oct 29 rule). Each change restarts the sample.
- **Stop and review (execution):** a compare mismatch that is not explained by a refusal and that repeats; live entries on candles the backtest did not trade (or the reverse); slippage clearly above 0.02%; MEXC 6002 refusals that keep coming (every missed trade is a backtest trade the account does not get).
- **Stop and review (performance):** drawdown past about 26R; rolling 50 trades below -14R. Longest losing streak on record is 20 trades.
- **Checkpoints:** about Dec 4 (about 1 month), about Feb 4 (3 months, about 54 trades), about May 4 (6 months, about 109 trades). At each: net R vs the table above; compare matches; fills and refusals.

## 6. Caveats

- Binance candles in the backtest, MEXC candles live: signals agree 96-98% (ZEC weakest).
- Live R per trade will lag the backtest a little (slippage, refusals). Lower the expectation, do not read the backtest as a promise.
- Oct 2026 in the backtest is partial (data ends Oct 21-26).
- Dollar values use R = 2.5% of balance (about $4.6-5 on the $185-198 BB-Core account of Oct 29); the risk steps down to 2% at $500 and 1.5% at $1,000.
