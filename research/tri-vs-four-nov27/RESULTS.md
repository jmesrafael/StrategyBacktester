# TRI-CORE vs 4 bots (+ BB 1H WIDE): ₱1,000 from Jan 2020 (Nov 27, 2026)

Coins: BB-CORE and WIDE = GALA SOL ADA NEAR ZEC ARB; I-CORE = GALA SOL ADA NEAR ZEC ETH; T-CORE = GALA SOL ADA NEAR ZEC ETH SUI ARB.

## Verdict

- **TRI-CORE ends higher:** ₱1,000 → ₱43.03M vs ₱36.55M with 4 bots (15% less), and it won 4 of 7 years.
- **The worst fall is the same:** 46.6% vs 47.2%; in R 70.1 vs 70.2. 4 bots is a little calmer along the way: losing months 37% vs 40%, deepest 12-month fall 51% vs 53%, and 12-month stretches that fell over 50%: 1% vs 21%.
- **Total R:** the whole account earns +865R with TRI-CORE and +835R with 4 bots. Added up bot by bot, 4 bots trade more (5,105 vs 3,789 trades), but each bot gets a smaller share, so the account total is lower.
- **Set to the same 40% worst fall, they tie:** 286% vs 288% a year. WIDE mostly replaces half of BB-CORE with a near copy (their months move together, link 0.84), so it adds trades, not protection.
- **Verdict: not worth adding the 4th bot now.** Same worst fall, 15% less money, a slightly smoother ride, more trades to manage and a 4th MEXC sub-account. The gain is too small to act on. Keep TRI-CORE and let WIDE run on paper until the late-February 2027 review.

## Read before trusting

- Live rules (BB-CORE v4 at 1.05x, I-CORE v3, T-CORE v3) and BB 1H WIDE 2.6σ; Binance candles + funding, fee 0.05% + slippage 0.02% per side; entries from Jan 1 2020, exits to Nov 24 2026, open trades valued every hour.
- One account with a sub-account per bot, sizing from the total times each bot's share, rebalanced every quarter, MEXC minimum orders (₱1,000 = US$16.67 at ₱60). Risk per trade 2.5%, 2% from a US$500 bot base, 1.5% from US$1,000.
- The money amounts are backtest compounding with perfect fills at any size. They are far above what to expect live. Use them only to compare the two lineups.
- The 71 twelve-month stretches overlap (about 7 separate years), so differences of a few percent are noise. The live rules were chosen in November 2026 on this same history.
