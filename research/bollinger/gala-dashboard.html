4-Coin vs 5-Coin System — fresh ₱10,000 every year

BB Breakout backtest, 1:4 target, Binance USDT-M futures 4h. Each calendar year starts a brand-new ₱10,000 account, no carryover. All trades from every coin in the system are merged in time order and sized off one shared account. Fixed risks a flat % of that year's starting balance on every trade; Compounding risks that % of whatever the balance has grown to.

With 4-5 coins live at once, 150-250+ trades fire per year. At 5% compounding risk that many trades compounds into the extreme year-end numbers below — they are what the math produces, not a realistic outcome: real slippage, fills, and correlated losing streaks would never let an account actually reach them. Read these as "which setting grows fastest in theory," not as a plan.
