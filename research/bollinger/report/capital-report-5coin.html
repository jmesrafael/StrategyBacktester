The rules in plain words

  • Looks at the 4-hour chart only. A trade starts when a candle closes outside the Bollinger Band (20, 2) in the direction of the 200 average (above it = long, below it = short).
  • Stop-loss = the middle band. Take-profit = 4 × that distance (1:4). Both are fixed at entry and never moved.
  • If neither is hit within 48 candles (8 days), the trade is closed at market.
  • One trade at a time per coin. Size is set so hitting the stop loses about $5.

What is included

  • Trading fees: 0.05% on market entry, stop and time exit; 0.02% on the take-profit limit order (Binance standard tier).
  • Slippage: 0.02% worse price on every market fill. The take-profit only counts as hit if price goes 0.05% through it.
  • Funding: the real Binance funding rate for every 8-hour payment the trade was open through.
  • Binance USDT-M 4h candles. No compounding: the risk stays $5 even as balances grow. Server / API costs are not included (API use itself is free).

Rules to set before going live

  • Expect a 20-trade losing streak and a −$128 drop on the $500 account at some point. Both are normal for this strategy.
  • Kill switch: stop the bot if the $500 account falls about 2× the worst drawdown from its high (≈ −$256). That would mean something changed.
  • $5 risk = 5% of each $100. A single coin can lose 54–78% of its starting $100 in its worst stretch — only run it with money you can leave alone for months.
  • Judge it on 6–12 months, not weeks. Roughly 1 month in 3 is a loss even when everything works.

Before you trust these numbers

  • These 5 coins were picked because they did well. Across ~90 coins the average result since 2023 was about a third of these coins'. Expect live results to be lower.
