# Pullback FVG — frozen settings on SOL / ZEC / DOGE

Frozen: FVG ≥ 0.5 ATR, limit at the gap's near edge, stop at the impulse origin, 3R target, cancel after 20 bars, 4h SMA200 trend filter. "Direct" = same but a market entry at the next open; "confirmation" = wait for a bar that dips into the gap and closes back with the trend, then a market entry. Costs 0.04% commission + 0.02% slippage per side; limit fills at the touch with no slippage (the app's rule). TRAIN = entries before 2023-01-01, TEST = 2023-01-01 on. R = amount risked.
