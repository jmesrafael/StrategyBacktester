What if some trades close early or late, at random?

Same strategy, but the sequence is shaken at random. A share of signals/trades (10%, 25%, 50%) is changed at random, 40 different random runs each, Jan 2020 to Nov 2026 on each bot's live coins, with fees, slippage and funding.

Opening: a chosen signal is skipped (1 in 3) or entered 1-12 candles late (2 in 3), with the stop and ATR re-read at the late entry. Because trades then open and close at different times, the later signals each coin can take also shift, so the whole sequence is different. Closing early: closed at a random candle 1-48 after entry (if the stop comes first, nothing changes). Closing late (BB-CORE only, the only bot with a time exit): the time exit is held 1-24 candles longer; the stop stays live. I-CORE and T-CORE have no time exit, so they only get early closes.

The rows: closing only, opening only, and opening + closing all random. Random draws never look at what price does next, so there is no hidden future sight. Green = better than today, red = worse.

Takeaway. Shaking when trades open and close never beats today's rules on average. Random openings alone are the mildest (T-CORE barely moves: -2% at 25%, -7% at 50%; BB-CORE -13% and I-CORE -11% at 25%). Random closing hurts more, and opening plus closing together hurts most: at 25% shaken BB-CORE loses about 16%, T-CORE 23%, I-CORE 28%; at 50% about 32%, 43% and 56%. In every run each bot stays profitable overall, but T-CORE and I-CORE can drop to 5 or 6 profitable years out of 7. The edge is not fragile to a bit of timing noise, but the live sequence is the best of the family. Source: research/situational-exits-nov24/random.js (research only, no bot changed).
