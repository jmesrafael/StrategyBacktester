1. The short answer

Too early to call a sustained rally. Only the first liquidity pool ($62K) has been swept. Price needs a daily close above ~$68–70K and ETF flows turning positive before the trend argument flips. The most likely path is a range of $60–68K with one more probe of $60–62K. The biggest risk to the shorts is a fast relief squeeze, because funding is negative and shorts are crowded.

Probabilities below are my own judgment from news and analyst commentary, not a model output. Treat them as rough weights.

2. Four paths for the next two weeks

Illustrative paths, not forecasts of exact prices.

A Chop with one more sweep  ·  ~45%

  • Range $60–68K. A probe of $60–62K to take stops and liquidations, then a bounce. Several analysts expect $62–67.7K this week.
  • Shorts: mostly flat to slightly better. Open profit holds. Good window to take partial profit near $60–62K.
  • Signal: lower highs under $68K, ETF flows still negative.

B Relief squeeze, then rejection  ·  ~30%

  • Negative funding after a $1.64B flush fuels a run to $68–72K. The 200-day MA near $73K caps it, and price rolls over again.
  • Shorts: the dangerous one. Alts can bounce 10–15%, and untrailed stops would hand back much of the open profit (see section 4).
  • Signal: daily close above $68K with rising ETF inflows.

C Capitulation to the summer low  ·  ~15%

  • $62K breaks on a daily close, then $60K, then $57.5K with a final liquidation cascade. The macro and flow headwinds (IPO cash demand, outflows) keep pressing.
  • Shorts: best case. This is where the largest gains show up, and also where a reversal is most violent. Don't hold everything into the low.
  • Signal: daily close below $62K, volume spike, funding flipping extreme.

D Reclaim and run  ·  ~10%

  • BTC reclaims $73K, the 200-day MA, on strong flows or a macro catalyst. The bear thesis is broken and shorts squeeze toward $76–82K.
  • Shorts: the loss scenario. This is the one the stops exist for.
  • Signal: daily close above $73K.
