# First 10 list indicators - 4h BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, ~7 years (2019-09 to 2026-11)

Generated by report.js from results.json, results-R2.json, baseline.json. Binance USDT-M 4h futures.

## 0. How this was tested (and why it cannot repaint or peek)

- **First 10 of the list** = the Trend section: SMA, EMA, WMA, VWMA, SMMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, KAMA, ALMA.
- **No look-ahead / no repaint.** Every indicator is causal. lookahead-check.js recomputed 300 points (2 coins x 10 indicators x 3 lengths x 5 bars) on data truncated at that bar: **0 mismatches**. Signals use the closed bar only and fill at the next open; stops are checked against the bar's high/low; an unknowable candle order is resolved stop-first.
- **Same trade loop as I-Core.** Stage 2 uses I-Core's trade loop and cost model; feeding I-Core's own signals through my loop reproduced its trades with max R difference 0.000000 (BTC 197 / ADA 176 trades).
- **Costs:** Stage 1 = 0.04% fee + 0.02% slippage per side (the app's rule). Stage 2 = I-Core model: 0.05% fee + 0.02% slip per side + 0.005% funding per 4h bar held. Not modelled: real funding history, liquidity limits.
- **Honest split:** TRAIN = trades closed before 2023-01-01 (dominated by the 2021 bull), TEST = 2023-01 to now (3.8y: chop, 2024 rally, 2025-26). Account maths = pooled trades of the 4 coins, 2.5% of equity risked per trade, compounded (as in the bots' own reports); drawdown on closed-trade equity.

## 5. Verdict

**Most profitable of the 10:** none stands alone - the 10 moving averages are near-interchangeable once tweaked (family median TEST expectancy 1.58R-1.86R; SMA/EMA/SMMA/KAMA/VWMA slightly ahead, ALMA/HMA slightly behind). The edge comes from the **tweaks**, not the formula:
1. SMA200 trend filter, 2. long-only, 3. tight 2-ATR stop, 4. very wide 10-20 ATR trailing stop, no target.

**Highest total R (full 7y):** champion VWMA close x MA200 + SMA200 filter, long-only, 2 ATR stop / 14 ATR trail = **+686R** (I-Core +449R, bb-core +425R). Top single configs reach 750-770R (ALMA 50/200, HMA 20, EMA 100) but those have drawdowns of -63% to -84% and are not picked here.
**Best risk-adjusted of the train-picked set (TEST):** WMA MA50xMA100 and ALMA MA50xMA200 (long-only, filter, 2/10 ATR): TEST ~+2R/trade, PF ~3.4, DD -46%..-60%, but only ~110-118 TEST trades, so noisy.

**Did it beat bb-core and I-Core?**
- On **profit per trade and total R: yes**, and not by cherry-pick - 90% of the 270 robust-zone configs beat I-Core's TEST total R, 84% beat bb-core's, 100% beat I-Core's TEST expectancy. Champion TEST: 1.95R/trade, PF 3.3, +338R vs I-Core +167R / bb-core +190R.
- On **smoothness: no.** Champion win rate is 20% (bots 30-34%), drawdown is slightly worse (-71% vs -69%/-64% at 2.5% risk; at 1% risk -39% vs -36%/-32%), and it made 635 of 686 R in just two years (2021: +341, 2024: +294); 2022 -36R, 2025 ~0R. bb-core was positive 8/8 years, I-Core 7/8, champion 5/8. The bots are the steadier machines; the MA trail-systems are the bigger but lumpier ones.
- Long+short variants picked on TRAIN mostly collapsed in TEST (EMA/SMMA 100: -0R to -3R, DD -89%) - shorts and loose filters are what to avoid.

**Caveats (read before trusting):** (1) long-only is a bet on crypto drift; 2019-2026 for BTC/ETH/SOL/ADA was net up; a long bear would hurt; (2) the 4 coins are highly correlated, so "4 coins" is closer to 1.5 independent bets; (3) TEST has only ~110-340 trades per config; (4) 4,320 configs were tried - the family-median evidence is what to believe, not the single best row; (5) funding is I-Core's flat 0.005%/bar, not real history; the first-bar fill/slippage is not liquidity-tested.

**Suggested next step:** since the champion wins in 2021/2024 trends and the bots win in other years (bb-core 2022-2023, 2026), test them as a **tandem** (like research/bb-core/no-target already does for BB + I-Core) - low correlation of yearly R suggests a smoother combined curve. Not done here.
